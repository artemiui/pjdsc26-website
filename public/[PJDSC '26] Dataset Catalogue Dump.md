2026 Dataset Catalog

Introduction   
The Philippine Junior Data Science Challenge (PJDSC) 2026 is your platform to turn raw numbers into real-world impact. This year, our focus shifts to Public Health Analytics with the theme "Trace the Pattern, Target the Cure," and we are thrilled to see the groundbreaking ideas you will bring to life\!  
To get your engines running, we’ve provided a collection of datasets to help you craft your initial concept proposals and fuel your final projects. These datasets will serve as your primary toolkit for analyzing critical issues like monitoring disease outbreaks, predicting community health risks, and optimizing healthcare delivery. Your analytical insights have the potential to drive better disease prevention, enhance early intervention strategies, and build stronger, healthier communities. While this catalogue is a great launching pad, feel free to use additional datasets beyond what's provided here\!

Adolescent Mortality Rate  
(per 100, age specific cohort)  
Worldwide data of adolescent mortality, per 1000 of each cohort (age range) per country per year.

* Groups by sex, and divides adolescents into three cohorts ranging from 10-19 years old  
* Data spans from 1991 to 2024  
* Filterable by location types

[https\://www\.who.int/data/gho/data/indicators/indicator-details/GHO/adolescent-mortality-rate-per-1000-age-specific-cohort](https://www.who.int/data/gho/data/indicators/indicator-details/GHO/adolescent-mortality-rate-per-1000-age-specific-cohort)

Foodborne deaths  
(mean, 95% uncertainty interval)  
Worldwide dataset containing mean amount of deaths arising from food consumption per year.

* Groups by age and types of hazards (e.g. virus, parasite, bacteria)  
* Dataset spans from 2000 to 2001

[https\://www\.who.int/data/gho/data/indicators/indicator-details/GHO/foodborne-deaths--2010-(mean--95--uncertainty-interval)](https://www.who.int/data/gho/data/indicators/indicator-details/GHO/foodborne-deaths--2010-\(mean--95--uncertainty-interval\))

Measles \- number of reported cases   
Simple dataset that record amount of reported measles cases per country per year.

* Dataset spans from 1974 to 2025  
* Filterable by location types

[https\://www\.who.int/data/gho/data/indicators/indicator-details/GHO/measles---number-of-reported-cases](https://www.who.int/data/gho/data/indicators/indicator-details/GHO/measles---number-of-reported-cases)

WASH basic drinking-water services, population using at least (%)   
Worldwide dataset that records percent of country population that has access to basic drinking-water services per year.

* Data is grouped by total, urban, and rural population percentages  
* Dataset spans from 2000 to 2024  
* Filterable by location types

[https\://www\.who.int/data/gho/data/indicators/indicator-details/GHO/population-using-at-least-basic-drinking-water-services-(-)](https://www.who.int/data/gho/data/indicators/indicator-details/GHO/population-using-at-least-basic-drinking-water-services-\(-\))

Air pollution: concentrations of fine particulate matter (PM2.5), SDG 11.6.2   
Dataset contains data of concentration of particulate matter with diameter less than 2.5 micrometers (PM2.5) per year.

* Grouped by total (country), urban, rural, cities, and towns  
* Dataset spans from 2010 to 2023  
* Filterable by location types.

https\://www\.who.int/data/gho/data/indicators/indicator-details/GHO/concentrations-of-fine-particulate-matter-(pm2-5)

Alzheimer's Disease and  
Healthy Aging Data  
Provided by the CDC Division of Population Health. Contains data from 2015-2022 on various topics related to Alzheimer’s Disease and Aging, such as smoking, cognitive decline, careiving, etc.   
[https\://data.cdc.gov/Healthy-Aging/Alzheimer-s-Disease-and-Healthy-Aging-Data/hfr9-rurv/about\_data](https://data.cdc.gov/Healthy-Aging/Alzheimer-s-Disease-and-Healthy-Aging-Data/hfr9-rurv/about_data) 

Tests for antibodies to trachoma PGP3 antigen   
This set includes data used in a latent class model to compare testing platforms for detection of antibodies against the Chlamydia trachomatis antigen Pgp3. Dataset is provided by the Division of Parasitic Diseases and Malaria.   
[https\://data.cdc.gov/Global-Health/Tests-for-antibodies-to-trachoma-PGP3-antigen/pwgb-7r9t/about\_data](https://data.cdc.gov/Global-Health/Tests-for-antibodies-to-trachoma-PGP3-antigen/pwgb-7r9t/about_data)

Disability and Health Data System (DHDS) \- Prevalence of Disability Status and Types   
Sourced from the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, National Center on Birth Defects and Developmental Disabilities, this dataset contains US (international) state-level data on adults with functional disabilities. 

[https\://data.cdc.gov/Disability-Health/DHDS-Prevalence-of-Disability-Status-and-Types/s2qv-b27b/about\_data](https://data.cdc.gov/Disability-Health/DHDS-Prevalence-of-Disability-Status-and-Types/s2qv-b27b/about_data)

COVID-19 DOH Data 

Dataset on COVID-19 situations in the a Philippines as of November 19, 2022 provided by the Department of Health-Epidemiology Bureau. 

[https\://data.gov.ph/index/public/dataset/COVID-19%20DOH%20Data%20Drop%20%28November%2019,%202022%29/zkn3dj3a-8q8j-3tn8-ybwc-wycq0elukpzp](https://data.gov.ph/index/public/dataset/COVID-19%20DOH%20Data%20Drop%20%28November%2019,%202022%29/zkn3dj3a-8q8j-3tn8-ybwc-wycq0elukpzp)

Tuberculosis incidence  
(per 100 000 population)  
Provided by the World Health Organizatio, this dataset contains number of new tuberculosis (TB) cases in a given year per 100,000 population.   
[https\://data.who.int/indicators/i/13B4226/C288D13?m49=608](https://data.who.int/indicators/i/13B4226/C288D13?m49=608)

U.S. Chronic Disease Indicators (CDI) 

This dataset provides national and state estimates for surveillance indicators of chronic diseases and their behavioral risk factors. 

[https\://www\.cdc.gov/cdi/index.html](https://www.cdc.gov/cdi/index.html)

Life Expectancy at Birth (Years) 

This global dataset tracks the average number of years a newborn is expected to live based on prevailing mortality rates. It is highly segmented by country, region, year, and sex. 

[https\://data.who.int/indicators/i/A21CFC2/90E2E48](https://data.who.int/indicators/i/A21CFC2/90E2E48)

Philippine Dengue Cases  
and Deaths  
This dataset records confirmed dengue cases and casualties aggregated by the DOH Epidemiology Bureau.   
[https\://data.humdata.org/dataset/philippine-dengue-cases-and-deaths](https://data.humdata.org/dataset/philippine-dengue-cases-and-deaths)

Joint Child Malnutrition Estimates (Stunting, Wasting, and Overweight)   
A global dataset that tracks the prevalence of child malnutrition, including modeled national data and survey estimates for stunting and wasting.   
[https\://data.unicef.org/topic/nutrition/malnutrition/](https://data.unicef.org/topic/nutrition/malnutrition/)

Global Maternal Mortality Estimates   
This internationally recognized dataset tracks maternal mortality ratios (MMR), total maternal deaths, and the lifetime risk of maternal death across more than 180 countries. It is a critical metric for assessing global health equity and healthcare access. 

[https\://data.unicef.org/resources/dataset/maternal-mortality-data/](https://data.unicef.org/resources/dataset/maternal-mortality-data/)
